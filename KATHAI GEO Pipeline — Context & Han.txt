KATHAI GEO Pipeline — Context & Handover
The goal
Make KATHAI's product pages win in AI-powered search and recommendation (GEO), and make the pages themselves adapt to the query. Two connected pieces:
1.  Refine AI ranking — improve how answer engines rank KATHAI flavour pages using structured FAQ, JSON-LD, richer imagery and better content.
2.  Query-personalized pages — given a user query (e.g. "I want hot chocolate with my family and need an experience"), identify the right product, regenerate its HTML page personalized to that intent, and show that this both (a) improves its ranking and (b) refreshes the page itself.
The worked example: a product ranked 5th for a query should reach rank 1 after its page is refreshed — competitors' pages untouched.
Source material
All content comes from two client-authored files (provenance: observed brand copy — no consumer-validated data exists anywhere in the project).

What was built
A working Python prototype (kathai_geo/, delivered as a zip in the chat) that runs end to end with python demo.py. The pipeline: parse the query into intent facets → score every live page → rank → pick the best-fit underperformer → regenerate only that page → re-rank.

Query ranking = visibility × (0.5 + 0.5 × intent_match). Intent facets (family, experience, gift, focus, calm, adventure, dessert, fitness, evening) match against product tags, best moments and attribute thresholds; the experience facet only credits a page that actually surfaces the Atelier.
The personalizer regenerates a page for a query: intent-led hero line and occasion block above the fold (e.g. family → the Atelier block), intent-relevant FAQs ranked first in the FAQPage schema, full JSON-LD (Product + FAQPage + Breadcrumb), canonical + Open Graph tags, six images with alt text, prep steps and serves.

Guardrails (kathai-provenance)
The project enforces the KATHAI provenance discipline throughout:
• Every claim carries a tier; no validated source is connected, so nothing emits a "validated" verdict. Scores are ordinal indices, never probabilities or conversion likelihoods; every ranking ships with readiness_caveat().
• No fabricated ratings or reviews — aggregateRating is deliberately absent from JSON-LD; the scorer zeroes claim-safety if any appears.
• No prices rendered — café pricing is a synthetic model (5,000 synthetic choice occasions), Atelier price is unconfirmed.
• Blocked claims — "high protein", "low sugar", "vegan", "preservative-free" stay off pages until the formulation qualifies; caffeine (Rush E) and protein grams (Macro Melt) are placeholders, so FAQs needing them are excluded rather than guessed.
• Scoring reads the rendered payload, not the intended render (rule 4: payload, not prompt).
• The seeded before-state is labelled a demo scenario, not a measured live site.

What the refresh added to the Salora page

  • Product JSON-LD — brand, images, SKU, ProductGroup variant link, taste attributes. Offers/price and ratings deliberately omitted: pricing is an unvalidated model and no reviews exist.
  • FAQPage JSON-LD — 8 quotable Q&As, with family/Atelier questions ranked first for this query. Questions needing unconfirmed formulation data (caffeine, protein) are excluded, not guessed.
  • Intent-led content — a family/Atelier occasion block (90-min session, 15 guests, Indiranagar) and a personalized hero line, above the fold.
  • Answerability blocks — taste scores table, OPEN→POUR→MELT prep, "Make it yours" serves.
  • Six-part visual system — pack, hero, ingredient story, mood, texture, prep — each with descriptive alt text.
  • Breadcrumb JSON-LD, canonical, Open Graph.

Provenance: all page copy is observed (brand-authored, Kathai_Flavours.docx); scores are inferred. Scores are ordinal indices derived from on-page structural signals and brand-authored copy (provenance: observed/inferred). No outcome or consumer-validated data exists; do not read these as probabilities, CTR predictions or conversion likelihoods. The "before" state is a seeded demo scenario, not a measured live site.

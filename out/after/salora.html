Questions, answered

Is Kathai good for a family outing?

Yes — the Kathai Atelier is a 90-minute guided hot chocolate session for up to 15 guests at 100 Feet Road, Indiranagar, Bengaluru, where your cup is made in front of you and you taste, compare and create a melt of your own.

Which flavour should we pick for a group?

Salora (sea salt caramel) is the crowd-favourite comfort pick; Solé and Moss suit explorers, and Red Noir suits dessert lovers — so a group can mix across the range.

What happens at the KATHAI Atelier?

A 90-minute guided session for up to 15 guests at KATHAI, 100 Feet Road, Indiranagar, Bengaluru that engages all five senses: Sight: Your cup is made in front of you, from pour to finish. Smell: Cocoa melting slowly, filling the room. Sound: A soft soundscape of santoor, bansuri, jal tarang, celesta and piano. Touch: A warm ceramic cup and a slow, velvety texture. Taste: Taste, compare and create a melt of your own.

Sweet + Salty + Smooth. Chocolate and caramel bring the indulgence. Sea salt cuts through the sweetness and keeps you coming back for another sip. Familiar enough to become a regular, interesting enough that it does not taste like one.

How sweet is Salora?

Sweetness is 4 out of 5, with chocolate intensity 4 out of 5 and a rich + velvety body.

Should I have Salora with milk or black?

Both work: have it with milk or black.

Can I drink Salora iced?

Yes — it can be served hot or iced.

When is the best moment for Salora?

Best enjoyed: anytime, dessert.
